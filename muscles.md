# Muscles of the Human Body

270 named skeletal muscles, plus 25 named smooth muscles and 5 cardiac muscle structures. Names are the English forms of Terminologia Anatomica (TA98/TA2), the international standard for anatomical terms.

Counting both sides of the body and each serial muscle (for example, 11 external intercostals per side), the skeletal entries add up to roughly **702** individual muscles (**646** if you leave out muscles that not everyone has). Published totals range from about 600 to 850 because sources count differently.

## How to read this list

- One entry per named muscle. Heads, bellies and parts appear under their muscle; they are not counted separately.
- Every muscle is paired (left and right) unless marked *unpaired*.
- *variable* means the muscle is not present in everyone.
- ×N is the number of such muscles on each side.

## Anatomical variants

### Well-documented extra muscles (not in Terminologia Anatomica)

- **Levator claviculae** *(variable)* — Neck; from the upper cervical vertebrae to the clavicle.
- **Axillary arch muscle** *(variable)* — Also called: Langer's axillary arch. Armpit; a slip from latissimus dorsi crossing the axilla.
- **Chondroepitrochlearis** *(variable)* — Chest to arm; a slip from pectoralis major toward the medial epicondyle.
- **Anconeus epitrochlearis** *(variable)* — Also called: Epitrochleoanconeus. Elbow; bridges the cubital tunnel over the ulnar nerve.
- **Gantzer's muscle** *(variable)* — Also called: Accessory head of flexor pollicis longus. Forearm; one of the most common forearm variants.
- **Extensor digitorum brevis manus** *(variable)* — Back of the hand; can be mistaken for a ganglion cyst.
- **Fibularis quartus** *(variable)* — Also called: Peroneus quartus. Outer ankle.
- **Accessory soleus** *(variable)* — Calf; can be mistaken for a soft-tissue mass.
